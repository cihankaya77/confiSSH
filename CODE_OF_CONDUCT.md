# ConfiSSH Code of Conduct

ConfiSSH welcomes users and contributors with different backgrounds and levels
of experience. Everyone should be able to ask for help, report a problem, or
contribute without harassment or discrimination.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and patient, including with people who are new to Linux or SSH.
- Discuss ideas and technical decisions without attacking the person proposing them.
- Give useful feedback, acknowledge mistakes, and accept reasonable corrections.
- Respect people's time, privacy, and boundaries.
- Use example data when sharing logs, screenshots, or configuration files.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks, sexual harassment, personal
attacks, and repeated unwanted contact are not acceptable. Neither are spam,
deliberate disruption, retaliation against someone who reports a concern, or
publishing another person's private information without permission.

Do not publish credentials, private keys, or other people's connection details.
For software vulnerabilities, follow the [security policy](SECURITY.md).

## Where this applies

These rules apply to this repository's issues, pull requests, reviews, and other
project-managed spaces. They also apply when someone represents ConfiSSH in a
public community.

## Reporting a concern

For a project moderation request that is safe to discuss publicly, open an
[issue](https://github.com/cihankaya77/confiSSH/issues) and mention
[@cihankaya77](https://github.com/cihankaya77). Include links to the relevant
content and a brief explanation. Keep personal and sensitive details out of
public reports.

For harassment, threats, or reports that need to stay private, use GitHub's
**Report content** option or [report abuse to GitHub](https://github.com/contact/report-abuse).
Those reports go to GitHub; they are not a private inbox for project maintainers.
See [GitHub's reporting guidance](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
for the available reporting routes.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove inappropriate content, request a correction, issue a
warning, restrict participation temporarily, or block a participant. The
response depends on the severity, impact, and repetition of the behavior;
serious abuse may lead to an immediate restriction.

Maintainers should apply these rules consistently, consider relevant context,
and limit disclosure of sensitive information. These expectations apply to
maintainers as well as other participants.
